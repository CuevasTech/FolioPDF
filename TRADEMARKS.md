# Folio PDF name and logo

**English** · [Español](#nombre-y-logo-de-folio-pdf)

Folio PDF is free software under the [GNU AGPL, version 3 or later](LICENSE). The licence lets you use, study, share and modify the code. It does not cover the name or the logo. As section 7(e) of the AGPL allows, it grants no rights to use the names **Folio** and **Folio PDF**, or the **Folio logo**, as trademarks. They identify the official Folio, made by CuevasTech.

### What you can do without asking
- Share the official, unmodified builds under the name Folio PDF. They are published at [github.com/CuevasTech/FolioPDF/releases](https://github.com/CuevasTech/FolioPDF/releases) and [foliopdf.org](https://foliopdf.org).
- Package the official builds for a package manager or a Linux distribution. Changes needed only to build Folio or to fit it into the system are fine, as long as the package says it is not official.
- Use the name to talk about Folio: articles, reviews, tutorials, comparisons, lists of alternatives.
- Say truthfully that your project is "based on Folio PDF", is "a fork of Folio PDF" or works with it.

### What needs your own name and logo
- **Any modified version or fork you distribute.** Give it a name and a logo that can't be confused with Folio, and don't present it as the official Folio or as an update to it.
- Don't use the name or the logo in a way that suggests that CuevasTech makes, endorses or supports your product.
- Don't use confusingly similar names, domains, app store listings or logos (for example "Folio PDF Pro" or "Folio PDF Free").

If you are not sure, ask at [contacto@foliopdf.org](mailto:contacto@foliopdf.org).
